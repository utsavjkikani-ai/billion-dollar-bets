Privacy policy

For BDB Uploader, the internal upload tool of the Billion Dollar Bets YouTube channel. Last updated 8 October 2026.

Who we are

Billion Dollar Bets is a YouTube channel about business history. Contact: utsavjkikani@gmail.com.

What the tool does

BDB Uploader uses YouTube API Services to manage videos on the Billion Dollar Bets channel only. It is used only by the channel owner and the owner's assistants. It is not available to the public.

What data it accesses

  • Through Google sign in (OAuth), the channel owner grants the tool permission to upload and manage videos on the owner's own YouTube channel.
  • The tool reads and writes only our own channel's data: our videos, their titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, captions, playlists, privacy and schedule settings, and the processing status of our uploads.
  • It does not access, collect or store data about any other YouTube user or viewer.

How data is stored and shared

  • The OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored privately in the owner's working environment and are used only to perform the actions above.
  • We do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • Video files and text we upload are our own content and are sent only to YouTube.

Google and YouTube policies

This tool uses YouTube API Services. See the Google Privacy Policy and the YouTube Terms of Service. You can revoke the tool's access at any time from the Google security settings page: security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.

Deleting data

Revoking access stops the tool immediately. Stored tokens are deleted when access is revoked or on request to utsavjkikani@gmail.com, within 7 days.

Changes

We will update this page if the tool changes what it does.
